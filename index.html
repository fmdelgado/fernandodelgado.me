03 · The bioinformatician

Signal, pulled from complexity.

From 2022 to 2025 I worked on network-medicine drug repurposing inside REPO4EU, the EU consortium (grant No. 101057619). I designed its prioritisation of host-directed antiviral candidates for HSV-1/2, evaluating 430 disease modules, and published with groups at TU Munich and Vienna, and with Harvard collaborators over the wider arc of the work.

Former Junior Group Leader for Generative AI for Medicine at the University of Hamburg, on a summa cum laude PhD and three master’s degrees. I review for Scientific Reports, PeerJ and the Journal of the Royal Society Interface, and have served as external examiner on three doctoral committees: Madrid, Granada and Seville.

Network medicine Multi-omics Drug repurposing Peer reviewer

04 · The AI engineer

Agents built to be checked.

In 2022 I wired PubMed retrieval into GPT-3 prototypes to curb hallucinations; that prototype became ChatDRex. At Galagos I owned the agent layer, the skill system (810 skills by June 2026), the evaluation grading and the end-to-end tests; my co-founder owned the platform. Retrieval is grounded in PubMed, UniProt and NCBI. My fix for tool-message streaming in ChatAnthropicVertex is merged upstream in langchain-ai/langchain-google (PR #1263).

AcadeMate Author

AI-assisted literature screening for systematic reviews: the applied companion to my first-author PNAS study, which benchmarked 18 LLMs against human reviewers across three reviews and reported a 33–93% reduction in one reviewer’s screening workload.

DRaCOoN Algorithm designer · First author

Pathway-level differential co-expression analysis in transcriptomics. I designed the algorithm and its Python package; the paper benchmarks an information-theoretic variant against eight alternative methods. BMC Bioinformatics (2025); on PyPI as dracoonpy.

EnGNet / pyEnGNet Algorithm creator · contributor
