03 · The bioinformatician & scientist

Signal, pulled from complexity.

From 2022 to 2025 I led network-medicine drug-repurposing initiatives inside REPO4EU, the EU consortium (grant No. 101057619), with an emphasis on infectious disease, building the multi-omics pipelines that turn disease modules into drug candidates, publishing with groups at TU Munich and Vienna, and with Harvard collaborators over the wider arc of the work.

Former Junior Group Leader for Generative AI for Medicine at the University of Hamburg, on a summa cum laude PhD and three master’s degrees. I review for Scientific Reports, PeerJ and the Journal of the Royal Society Interface, and have served as external examiner on three doctoral committees: Madrid, Granada and Seville.

Network medicine Multi-omics Drug repurposing Peer reviewer

04 · The AI engineer

Agentic systems that do real science.

I built my first conversational agent for disease-module discovery and drug repurposing in 2022, the direct precursor of ChatDRex, before “agentic” was a category. As CTO I led the architecture that grew out of it: model-agnostic multi-agent orchestration, a library of 250+ reusable agent skills, retrieval grounded in PubMed, UniProt and NCBI, and the evaluation harness we measured all of it against. My fix for tool-message streaming in ChatAnthropicVertex is merged upstream in langchain-ai/langchain-google (PR #1263).

07 · Selected work

01

Galagos Co-Founder · CTO

An agentic “virtual bioinformatician” I carried from prototype to benchmarked release as CTO. On the full 205-task BixBench set it solved 166 of 205, 81.0%, at roughly €0.87 a question, and 92.0% on the 50-question expert-verified subset (46 of 50). Both runs were scored in-house, with the methodology published openly. NVIDIA Inception member; approximately €234K in non-dilutive public grants.
